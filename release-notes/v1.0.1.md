### Your diet profile survives closing the app

Weight, height, age, activity level and the calorie target you set in **Diet
Profile** are now kept between launches. They were stored in a session cookie,
which Android discards when the app process ends — so the HP bar quietly fell
back to a 2000 kcal target and the profile looked wiped, even though the food
logs themselves were intact. Theme, accent colour and player name persist too.

### The app reopens on the right day

The date you were viewing is remembered as well. Closed across midnight, or for
a few days, the app now saves that day properly before moving on to today,
instead of showing yesterday's meals under today's date and saving them there.

### The day log is written when the app is backgrounded

Meals reach the day log when the app is hidden or killed, not only on the unload
event Android usually skips. The 14-day charts and **Export backup** therefore
include the meal you just added.

### Upgrading from 1.0.0

Install straight over 1.0.0 — your data is kept. One caveat: the profile saved
by 1.0.0 lived in a session cookie, so it is already gone by the time you
update. Enter it once more (or **Import** a backup) and it will stick from then
on.
